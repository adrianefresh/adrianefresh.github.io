TEACHING INTERESTS

I am passionate about teaching students to think critically about social and political phenomena through rigorous empirical analysis. My courses emphasize methodological rigor and the integration of historical context with quantitative approaches to understanding institutional change and political development. I am committed to creating inclusive learning environments where students from diverse backgrounds can engage with complex ideas and develop their own research skills. I believe that effective teaching requires clear communication, active engagement with student questions, and a willingness to adapt pedagogical approaches to meet diverse learning needs. I aim to inspire students to pursue their own research questions and to equip them with the tools—both theoretical and methodological—necessary to conduct meaningful scholarship that contributes to our understanding of politics, institutions, and social change.
