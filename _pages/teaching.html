TEACHING INTERESTS

I teach about political institutions, policing and incarceration, research design, causal inference, and practical approaches to doing data science. I'm passionate about teaching students how to ask good questions, evaluate evidence, and build something of their own. This translates into my graduate mentoring as well. I try to ask questions that cultivate independent judgment, offer candid criticism, and attend to the person behind the work.
I also co-direct Duke's Social Science Scholars Program (SoSci Scholars), which supports undergraduate research across the social and behavioral disciplines.
