excerpt: "This course is a graduate-level introduction to conducting research in political science. In the course, we focus on selected topics in the philosphy of science, question asking, theory building, and empirical research design, including designs for causal inference, selecting cases, and designing archival and field research.  Though we can't cover every topic in research design, students should leave the class with foundational skills and an understanding of what they don't know, but could learn more about.  In addition to the philosophical and conceptual work, students design and present a complete research proposal by the end of the term."
