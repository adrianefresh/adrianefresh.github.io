excerpt: "I informally title this course *Understanding Institutions to Change the World*.  This course is an undergraduate seminar that introduces students to the mechanics of specific institutions; theories of institutional origin, evolution and change; and asks students to engineer their own institution to solve a real-world problem that matters to them.  Students consider both major works in institutional theory (e.g., North, Greif, Bicchieri), and readings on specific examples of institutions (e.g., prison gangs, car-parking, foot-binding, peace accords, and corruption)."
