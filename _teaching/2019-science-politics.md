excerpt: "This course answers the question of why political science is a *science*.  The course answers this question by introducing students to the systematic study of politics.  In the course, we investigate subtantive questions and problems in the areas of of governance, poverty and war.  We ask why democracy and autocracy are distributed globally and historically in the way that they have been; we ask why some countries redistribute wealth more or less than others; and we ask why wrs are fought instead of being solved with a bargain.  Along the way, we introduce key concepts and methods in political science.  These include wht it means to build theories and generate hypotheses, how we test hypotheses with evidence, and how we use that evidence as a basis for policy recommendations."
