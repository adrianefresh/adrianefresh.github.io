excerpt: "This course was taught for an accredited prison university program--Mount Tamlpais College (formerly the Prison University Project)--in San Quentin Prison in California. This course introduces students to the process and problems of research, broadly defined.  At the same time, students improve their critical reading, critical thinking and writing skills in preparation for more advanced coursework. The course examines research in both theory and practice--from what it means to search for fact (or truth ) across different disciplines--to the methods that researchers employ in their work. Our readings will focus on completed works of academic research, texts about the research process, as well as texts that allow us to improve our written communication skills. Through these readings and a series of guest presentations by practicing researchers in a variety of fields, we’ll ask ourselves what questions interest researchers in different disciplines, what problems or concerns arise in their research, and how they and their discipline search for fact, or truth. How, in essence, do they go about knowing what they know?"
