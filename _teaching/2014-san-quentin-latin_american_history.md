excerpt: "This course was taught for an accredited prison university program--Mount Tamlpais College (formerly the Prison University Project)--in San Quentin State Prison in San Quentin, California. This course is a survey of Latin American history.  It briefly considers the colonial period, and then spends most of its time on the modern era since independence. Drawing from primary source documents and the writings of historians, students explore the role of race, class, and gender in the formation of nations, and consider the ideas of “progress” and “backwardness,” “civilization” and “barbarism” in the political, economic, intellectual, and cultural development of the region. Through a comparative approach, the class provides students a broad understanding of the origins and the historical trends shaping modern Latin American states."
