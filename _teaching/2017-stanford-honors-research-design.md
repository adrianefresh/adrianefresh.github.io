excerpt: "How do political scientists know what they know?  How can you apply these strategies to answering a question about the political world that you are curious about?  This course is an introduction to research design in political science for honors students.  Students learn to evaluate the scientific research through the written work of other scholars. Students also develop a research proposal of their own---beginning with identifying a research question that is answerable and worth answering, reading the existing scholarly literature, and proposing a plan for answering that question."
